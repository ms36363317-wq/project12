Abstract

Retinal diseases are a major cause of vision impairment and blindness worldwide, making early diagnosis essential for effective treatment. This project presents an intelligent retinal disease detection system based on deep learning and explainable artificial intelligence. The proposed system classifies retinal fundus images into seven categories: Diabetic Retinopathy, Disc Edema, Myopia, Pterygium, Retinal Detachment, Retinitis Pigmentosa, and Healthy. EfficientNetB3 was selected as the primary classification model due to its high accuracy and computational efficiency. Image preprocessing techniques, including resizing, normalization, and data augmentation, were applied to improve model performance. To enhance model transparency, Grad-CAM was integrated to visualize the retinal regions that influenced the model's predictions. In addition, a Large Language Model (LLM) was incorporated to generate medical explanations, including disease descriptions, symptoms, severity levels, and recommended actions. The system was implemented using Python, TensorFlow, OpenCV, Streamlit, and Ollama. Experimental results achieved an accuracy of 97.27%, precision of 97.32%, recall of 97.27%, F1-score of 97.27%, and an AUC of 0.9983. These results demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed system in providing accurate, explainable, and user-friendly retinal disease diagnosis, with the potential to support ophthalmologists and improve access to early screening services.

Keywords: Retinal Disease Detection, Deep Learning, EfficientNetB3, Grad-CAM, Explainable AI, Large Language Models, Medical Image Analysis.
